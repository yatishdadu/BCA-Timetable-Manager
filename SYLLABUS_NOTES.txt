=====================================
The optional import uses weekly teaching hours from BCA (General) course-structure
summary tables in the three user-provided Bengaluru City University syllabus PDFs:
- I & II semester structure, SEP 2024–25 (later BCA General summary table is used for codes).
- III & IV semester structure, SEP 2025–26, pages 4–5.
- V & VI semester structure, SEP 2026–27, pages 4–6 in the source document.

Imported subject counts and total WEEKLY SECTION HOURS (NOT faculty totals):
  I:   9 subjects, 31 h/week per section
  II: 10 subjects, 33 h/week per section
  III:9 subjects, 31 h/week per section
  IV: 10 subjects, 33 h/week per section
  V:   8 subjects, 32 h/week per section

These numbers are not faculty workload totals. Faculty workload depends on how many
sections are active, which subjects are assigned to the department's staff, and whether
project/internship supervision hours are counted according to college policy.

SOURCE CONFLICT / SCHEDULING EXCEPTIONS TO REVIEW
1. Lab courses in III, IV, V and VI have 3 hours/week in the course structure,
   while the college's operational rule requires continuous two-period lab sessions.
   This cannot be fulfilled with only identical 2-period blocks without adjustment;
   the software flags it instead of inventing a one-period lab.
2. The Semester VI internship overview lists "03*" guidance/supervision hours,
   while its later detailed internship page lists 02 hours/week.
   The import uses the overview figure (3h) but LABELS this for confirmation.
3. Languages L1/L2 are placeholders to rename, not assertions about which language
   or guest faculty will be active in a term.
4. Subject names and code revisions should be compared to the college's final HOD-
   approved course structure. The importer is optional and skips existing codes.
5. Subject allocations are per section. Total faculty "allocated hours" is the
   sum of assignments for that faculty IN ONE SELECTED TERM, not an approved clash-
   free timetable. Shared/combined teaching and split lab batches need later rules.
6. The 158-hour figure was explicitly excluded by the user and is not used.
